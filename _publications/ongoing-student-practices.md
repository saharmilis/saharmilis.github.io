## About

Existing evaluations rely mostly on single-semester snapshots or controlled laboratory tasks.
We present a multi-cohort study of 600 students, across 6 semesters, 3 academic years of the same university course.
Combining chatbot use with ongoing course analysis, lecture and recitation transcripts, attendance, assignments, and project submissions.
We examine whether authentic academic achievement is associated with the choice of tool or with learning practices - findings are translated into evidence-based guidance for future students.
