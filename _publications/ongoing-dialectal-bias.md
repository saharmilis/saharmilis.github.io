## About

Bias is everywhere; it is overt and unmistakable, but more often it is subtle and hidden.

In the preprocessing phase of large texts, standard flat deduplication creates severe gaps by systematically wiping out the subtle variations of minority dialects, heavily favoring the high-volume dominant language variant.
