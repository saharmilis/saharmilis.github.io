## About

Construction Decision Model is a specialized, non-autoregressive AI system designed to analyze visual evidence alongside textual contexts.

Unlike traditional generative AI that creates conversational text token by token, this architecture processes the multimodal inputs in a single forward pass to instantly return structured classifications, binary decisions, or scale ratings.
