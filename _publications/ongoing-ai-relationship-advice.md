## About

People increasingly turn to AI chatbots for relationship advice, yet there is little systematic evidence on the quality of that advice.
We evaluate multiple chatbots on various relationship-conflict scenarios using Gottman's Four Horsemen (criticism, contempt, defensiveness, stonewalling)
and their antidotes as a theory-grounded framework.
We assess whether the advice discourages the Horsemen, models the antidotes, or instead validates and reinforces destructive patterns, and examine how this varies across models, scenario types, and how users frame the conflict.
